FAQ

Questions

How long does delivery take?

UK orders dispatch from local stock within 2–5 working days (standard UK delivery 3–5 working days; express 1–2 working days). Shipping is free on UK orders £25+.

Can I return or exchange?

Yes. In line with 14-day UK statutory returns, unworn, unwashed pairs in original packaging can be returned or exchanged for another size.

Where does the 10% go?

Directly to partner vets for street cats in Cairo and the UK.

What are they made of?

200N combed cotton blended with elastane for stretch recovery, Oeko-Tex certified.

How do I wash them?

Machine wash 30°C inside out. Do not tumble dry hot; do not bleach.

Will Batch 01 restock?

No. Batch 01 is a limited archive; future batches will have new editions.
